# The Presocratic Reader

By Sergiu Alexandru Bîsceanu.

A reader for the Presocratic fragments in Diels–Kranz order, pairing original-language witnesses with published historical translations. English is preferred; German, French, Italian and Spanish extend the selection where English is unavailable. The reader includes Greek-word lookup in LSJ, text and concordance searches, translation comparison, and notes on the surviving sources. Its aim is to bring the wider corpus within reach, including material useful to readers of several languages.

Translations, editions, digital sources and artwork are credited in the reader. See [texts and licences](site/attribution/index.html) and [third-party notices](site/licences.txt). Corrections and source enquiries are welcome through this repository’s Issues.
