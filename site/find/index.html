How are matches chosen?

The search compares your description with short passages from the included English translations. It returns only candidates above a minimum similarity threshold. When none reaches that threshold, it reports “Zero matches found”.

A suggestion is a passage to inspect, not a verified identification of the saying you remember. Similar ideas may occur in several fragments. The result shows the published wording and links to its original text, translator and source.

This search does not compose quotations or translate the corpus. Passages available only in another language are outside its scope. For names, DK references and words in other languages, use .
