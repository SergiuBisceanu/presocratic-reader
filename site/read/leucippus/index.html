DK 67 B 2

Available translations: English

οὐδὲν χρῆμα μάτην γίνεται, ἀλλὰ πάντα ἐκ λόγου τε καὶ ὑπ’ ἀνάγκης.

Naught happens for nothing, but everything from a ground and of necessity.

John Burnet · Early Greek Philosophy, second edition (1908)
