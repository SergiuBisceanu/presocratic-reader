DK 75 B 3

Available translations: English

καθάπερ Ν. τὴν ἀκαταπληξίαν· ταύτην γὰρ ἔφη ὑπὸ Δημοκρίτου ‘ἀθαμβίην‘ λέγεσθαι

as Nausiphanes, that it was undauntedness, for he said that it was this that was called by Democritus imperturbability.

William Wilson · The Writings of Clement of Alexandria, vol. 2 (Edinburgh: T. & T. Clark, 1869)

DK 75 B 4LATIN WITNESS

Available translations: French

N. ait ex his quae videntur esse nihil magis esse quam non esse.

Nausiphane prétend que de ce qui semble être, il n’y a rien dont l’être soit plus constant que le non être.

French · Joseph Baillard (1799–1862) · Sénèque, Œuvres complètes, traduction Joseph Baillard, tome II, Hachette, 1914 (translation first published 1861)
